### Full Description (Max 4000 Characters)
```text
Experience the divine heritage of Braj Mandal with Braj Darshan — the ultimate mobile guide to sacred temples, shrines, and utsavs across Vrindavan, Mathura, Goverdhan, Barsana, Nandgaon, Gokul, and Radha Kund.

Whether you are embarking on the sacred Braj 84 Kos Yatra or seeking daily darshan information, Braj Darshan brings authentic temple history, darshan timings, high-resolution photo galleries, visitor amenities, and interactive map directions directly to your device.

KEY FEATURES:

🏛️ UNLIMITED TEMPLE EXPLORATION
Explore iconic shrines including Shri Banke Bihari Temple, Prem Mandir, ISKCON Vrindavan, Shri Radha Raman, Shri Radha Vallabh, Dwarkadhish Temple Mathura, Radha Rani Temple Barsana, and hidden heritage shrines.

⏰ DARSHAN TIMINGS & VISITOR INFORMATION
Access accurate morning and evening darshan timings, aarti schedules, contact information, and average visit durations to plan your pilgrimage effortlessly.

🖼️ HIGH-RESOLUTION CLOUDINARY GALLERIES
Browse pristine photo galleries with captions and full-screen pinch-to-zoom capabilities powered by Cloudinary auto-compressed WebP delivery.

🗺️ INTERACTIVE MAPS & OPENSTREETMAP DIRECTIONS
Locate nearby temples with real-time GPS positioning, interact with OpenStreetMap markers, and get turn-by-turn directions.

❤️ OFFLINE SAVED SHRINES
Save your favorite temples locally for offline viewing. No account registration or internet access required to view saved temple information.

🎉 CULTURAL FESTIVALS & UTSAVS
Stay informed about major celebrations including Krishna Janmashtami, Radhashtami, Lathmar Holi, Jhulan Yatra, and Annakut Mahotsav.

♿ VISITOR FACILITIES & ACCESSIBILITY
Filter temples by parking availability, wheelchair accessibility, prasad availability, and visitor accommodations.

🎨 ELEGANT MONOCHROME DESIGN
Enjoy a serene, distraction-free Material 3 minimal user interface with light and dark theme support tailored for comfortable reading day or night.

Braj Darshan is non-profit focused, respects pilgrim privacy, and requires no account creation or personal data input.

Jai Jai Shri Radhe!
```
